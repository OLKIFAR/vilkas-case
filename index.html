How scoring works
  • The problem: the real cause scores 10, the symptom scores 5.
  • The fix: the best fix scores 10, a half fix scores 5.
  • Finish early: +5 if you lock with 5:00 or more left, +3 with 3:00 or more. It only counts if your problem is right or half right.
  • A 6th clue costs 2 points. Overtime costs 2 points, and the case closes by itself at 13:00.
  • Best possible score: 25.
